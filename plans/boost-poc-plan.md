# Boost PoC – Voice Bot Use Case Plan

## Background

Based on the meeting transcription from June 17, 2026, the team agreed to build a comprehensive list of Voice Bot use cases for the Boost PoC. The focus has been narrowed to **real-time voice translation** for merchant services in the Nordics and DAC regions.

The list is generic enough to reuse across other platform evaluations (Genesis, Sinch, Found Ever).
